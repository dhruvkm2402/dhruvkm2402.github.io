## A few things I'm interested in talking about

- Simulation-first development: digital twins, scenario testing, and how much of a real deployment you can genuinely de-risk before hardware.
- Sim-to-real transfer that holds up — domain randomization, system identification, and knowing when your simulator is lying to you.
- Reinforcement learning for ground vehicles and multi-robot coordination.
- Getting modern perception and VLM/VLA models running fast enough on Jetson-class hardware to be useful.
- Teaching and explaining this work to people who don't already speak robotics.
